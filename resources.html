Resources you can open quietly

Support without a long search.

These links are here for different kinds of moments: immediate danger, texting instead of talking, finding ongoing care, or learning what support can look like.

If this is urgent

Use live support. A website can help you find the door, but another person should be involved when safety is at risk.

Call or text

988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline

In the U.S., call or text 988 for crisis support. You can also use their online chat.

Call 988 Visit 988
Text support

Crisis Text Line

Text HOME to 741741 in the U.S. to connect with a trained crisis counselor by text.

Text HOME Learn more
Emergency

Local emergency help

If someone may be hurt soon, call emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department.

Before you close the page

If you opened a resource but did not reach anyone, try one more live option or ask someone near you to stay. You do not have to make it sound perfect.

A small note

Some links may open away from this site. If a page feels like too much, you can come back here and start with one quiet step.

init.self gathers support links in one calm place. It is not a replacement for emergency care, therapy, or medical advice.
