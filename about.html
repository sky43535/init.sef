About

A small page for hard moments.

init.self is a few calm words and trusted links in one place. It is not a crisis service.

Please read this part

This site is made by people who care, not by mental health professionals. It cannot diagnose, treat, or replace a counselor, therapist, doctor, or crisis line. If you may be in danger, contact live help.

What it does

  • Offers simple grounding steps.
  • Links to crisis and mental health resources.
  • Gives you words to use when asking for help feels hard.

What it does not do

  • Give medical or clinical advice.
  • Ask you to share your story or personal details.
  • Promise secrecy when someone's safety is at risk.

Privacy

  • No accounts, posting, or forms.
  • No ads, analytics, or trackers added by us.
  • Text is set in Google Fonts, loaded from Google, and like any site, our host may keep basic server logs.

Who made it

init.self is a small independent project by Skysminds. It is a place to start, not a cure.

init.self is not professional care or a crisis service.
