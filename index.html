init.self

A calm space for the moments when being here feels heavy. Find a grounding exercise, a crisis line, a way to tell someone, or a next step that does not ask you to explain everything at once.

Find support now Try the 10-minute rule
If you may hurt yourself or someone else, get live help now. In the U.S., call or text 988, use 988 chat, or call emergency services. Outside the U.S.? Find a helpline in your country.

The 10-minute rule

Do not decide the whole future while the wave is high.

Set a small window: breathe, make the space safer, contact one person, and wait with support. When ten minutes pass, choose the next ten.

This space is here when you need it. You can leave, return, and only read what helps.

For someone supporting a friend

  • Stay with them if they feel unsafe being alone.
  • Use direct, calm language and ask if there is immediate danger.
  • Bring in a trusted adult, counselor, guardian, clinician, or emergency service when safety is at risk.
  • Do not promise secrecy around serious safety concerns.

For later, when things are quieter

  • Write a short safety plan: who to call, what helps, how to make your space safer.
  • Book a check-in with a counselor, doctor, therapist, or trusted person.
  • Keep it short enough to use when focus is low.
